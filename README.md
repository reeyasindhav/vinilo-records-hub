Problem Statement:
Vinyl record enthusiasts and collectors often struggle to buy, sell, and trade records safely online because generic e-commerce platforms lack specialized tools for music collectors. Most general marketplaces do not offer transparent, standardized condition grading systems, making it difficult for buyers to verify the true physical and audio quality of a record before purchasing. Additionally, finding specific pressings and genres is cumbersome due to inadequate filtering. Vinilo addresses these issues by providing a dedicated marketplace featuring transparent condition badges, rich genre filtering, and detailed seller profiles to create a trustworthy, collector-focused trading experience.
